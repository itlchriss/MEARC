Given an integer array param_nums, find three numbers whose product is maximum and the result is the maximum product.
Example 1:
Input: nums = [1,2,3]
Output: 6
Example 2:
Input: nums = [1,2,3,4]
Output: 24
Example 3:
Input: nums = [-1,-2,-3]
Output: -6
Constraints:
<code>3 <= nums.length <= 10<sup>4</sup></code>
`-1000 <= nums[i] <= 1000`
